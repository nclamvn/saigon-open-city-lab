Copernicus DEM - Global and European Digital Elevation Model

Digital Surface Model (DSM) provided in 3 different resolutions (90m, 30m, 10m) with varying geographical extent (EEA: European and GLO: global) and varying format (INSPIRE, DGED, DTED).

Copernicus DEM - Global and European Digital Elevation Model

The Copernicus DEM is a Digital Surface Model (DSM) that represents the surface of the Earth including buildings, infrastructure and vegetation. The Copernicus DEM is provided in 3 different instances: EEA-10, GLO-30 and GLO-90.

Data were acquired by the TanDEM-X mission between 2011 and 2015. If any Copernicus DEM products show a sensing date earlier than 2011, this is because older elevation models were used to fill data gaps. To make sure you find all available data in the Copernicus Browser, it is recommended to extend the search time range back to 2001. The datasets were made available for use in 2019 and will be maintained until 2026.

All registered users (i.e. How to register to access Copernicus Contributing Missions data) can freely access the 30m and 90m resolution instances of the Copernicus DEM.
Access to the 10m resolution instances over Europe is limited to a specific subset of users, as outlined in the ESA-User license for the use of CCM data and CCM Data Access Restrictions.

The Copernicus DEM is a Digital Surface Model (DSM) which represents the top-reflective surface of the Earth including buildings, infrastructure and vegetation. This DEM is derived from an edited DSM named WorldDEMTM , i.e. flattening of water bodies and consistent flow of rivers has been included. Editing of shore- and coastlines, special features such as airports and implausible terrain structures has also been applied.
In contrast to Digital Surface Models such as Copernicus DEM, a Digital Terrain Model (DTM) represents the Earth's terrain with buildings, infrastructure and vegetation being removed in a DEM editing process.
Additionally, hybrid DEMs are possible with e. g. only vegetation or only buildings / infrastructure removed.

Further information on DEM types is available in Peter Guth et.al.: Digital Elevation Models: Terminology and Definitions, 2021. (https://www.mdpi.com/2072-4292/13/18/3581)
The aforementioned differentiation should be considered when comparing DSMs, such as the current Copernicus DEM, with external reference data (e.g. LiDAR-based DTM data).

EEA-10: European coverage

EEA-10 covers 39 European countries including all islands of those countries plus French Overseas Departments (excluding French Overseas Territories) at a resolution of 10 metres with a surface coverage of ~ 6 M km 2 .

Land surface coverage within Copernicus DEM – EEA-10

GLO-30 and GLO-90: worldwide coverage

GLO-30 offers global coverage at a resolution of 30 metres. GLO-90 offers global coverage at a resolution of 90 metres. Both datasets have a surface coverage of ~ 149 M km 2 .

Land surface coverage within Copernicus DEM – GLO-30 & GLO-90

Nine releases are currently available for all Copernicus DEM instances. See in “Releases” section of this page for more details.

How to access the data

The following registered users can download all instances of the Copernicus DEM:

  • Public authorities
  • Copernicus Services
  • EU institutions
  • Space research projects (EU funded)
  • Non-space research projects (EU funded)
  • Copernicus Operators

The following registered users can view all instances of the Copernicus DEM. They can download the GLO-90 and the GLO-30 instances of the Copernicus DEM:

  • International organizations
  • General public

More details on the Copernicus DEM eligibility and access conditions can be found in the ESA User License and relevant Mission Specific Annex.

The Copernicus DEM is accessible via the Copernicus Browser or API. For further guidance and technical support, please visit the documentation portal (e.g. the Copernicus DEM section) or see the CDSE Forum. For issues/bugs please submit a request. Full dataset downloads (bulk access) are supported via S3-compatible object storage and OData API using temporary credentials. For more information please consult the related FAQ item: How can I download Copernicus data in bulk mode?.

WorldDEMTM infilled on a local basis with the following DEMs: ASTER, SRTM90, SRTM30, SRTM30plus, GMTED2010, TerraSAR-X Radargrammetric DEM, ALOS World 3D-30m, Norway National DEM and Spanish National DEM.

Format

The Copernicus DEM instances vary in format (INSPIRE, DGED, DTED) and resolution (0.3 to 3.0 arc seconds).

The Copernicus DEM coverage is available as a grid in ESRI .shp format:

  • The EEA-10 INSPIRE grid shapefile package (one per projection) is available here.
  • The GLO-90, GLO-30 and EEA-10 DGED grid shapefile is available here.

For each geocell, the attributes report the name of the associated Copernicus Product Packages (CPP) as they are stored in the CDSE catalogue together with the name of the products following the DTED naming convention. The attributes also include information about the rationale that triggered a specific geocell update ("Update" Type). The grid was created by Airbus Defence and Space GmbH as complementary information to the Copernicus DEM datasets.

Horizontal resolution and vertical accuracy

Grid spacing in latitude direction

  • EEA-10: 0.3" (INSPIRE) 0.4" (DGED);
  • GLO-30: 1.0" (DGED) 1.0" (DTED);
  • GLO-90: 3.0" (DGED) 3.0" (DTED).

Absolute vertical accuracy

  • < 4m (90% linear error)

Relative vertical accuracy

  • < 2m for slopes below or equal 20%;
  • < 4m for slopes above 20%;
  • (90% linear point-to-point error within an area of 1° x 1°)

Absolute horizontal accuracy

  • < 6m (90% circular error)

Processing/ Product quality/ aux. data

  • File Format: GeoTIFF and DTED
  • File Data Type:
    • 32 Bit, floating (DGED & INSPIRE format)
    • 16 Bit, signed (DTED format)
  • Tile Extent:
    • 1°x1° latitude/longitude (DGED & DTED format)
    • 100 kmx100 km (INSPIRE format)
  • Projection:
    • Geographic Coordinates (DGED & DTED format)
    • Projected Coordinates (INSPIRE format)
  • Coordinate Reference System:
    • Horizontal
      • WGS84-G1150 (EPSG 4326) (DGED & DTED format)
      • (EPSG 4326) for continental Europe (INSPIRE format)
      • UTM (EPSG 32740, 32622, 32738, 32620) for the French DOMs (INSPIRE format)
    • Vertical
      • EGM2008 (EPSG 3855)
  • Vertical Unit: meters
  • Grid alignment:
    • RasterPixelIsPoint
    • RasterPixelIsArea (INSPIRE format only)

Copernicus DEM quality layers (data format and type)

  • Editing Mask: 8 Bit unsigned integer, GeoTIFF
  • Filling Mask: 8 Bit unsigned integer, GeoTIFF
  • Height Error Mask: 32 Bit floating point, GeoTIFF
  • Water Body Mask: 8 Bit unsigned integer, GeoTIFF
  • Source Data Layer: KML vector file
  • Accuracy Layer: KML vector file

The methods and implementation of statistical procedures used to validate vertical accuracy of the Copernicus DEM are described in the Copernicus DEM validation report.

Metadata

  • INSPIRE compliant metadata.

File Format

  • GeoTIFF (fully compliant with TIFF version 6.0) and DTED

The Copernicus DEM has undergone the following improvements throughout the years:

  • Infilling with high resolution DEM over Norway and Spain;
  • Improvement of infilling algorithm;
  • Addition of 5 geocells containing missing small islands;
  • Editing of source raw data; 
  • Correction of minor data/auxiliary files inconsistencies;
  • Correction of implausible values.

Ancillary dataset to COP DEM EEA-10
The Copernicus Digital Elevation Model has been extended with two new data layers serving as an ancillary dataset to COP DEM EEA-10:

  • Minimum Amplitude (AM2)
  • Mean Amplitude (AMP)

These amplitude layers complement existing quality layers like the Height Error Map (HEM) and Water Body Mask (WBM) by providing additional information on SAR backscatter intensity.
With the addition of AM2 and AMP, the Copernicus DEM is further enhanced for several applicators such as:

  • Water body mapping
  • Land surface classification
  • Environmental monitoring

Both layers are available in the CDSE catalogue under the dataset COP-DEM_AMP-12-DGED, and are available for search and download via both the OData and S3 interfaces.

For example, the following OData query performs a search on these products:

catalogue.dataspace.copernicus.eu/odata/v1/Products?$filter=Attributes/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/any(att:att/Name eq %27datasetFull%27 and att/OData.CSC.StringAttribute/Value eq %27COP-DEM_AMP-12-DGED%27)&$expand=Attributes

These new data are available for download to users belonging to the following categories:

  • Copernicus Services and Union Institutional Users
  • Union Research Projects
  • National Public Authorities
  • Copernicus Operators.

Acceptance of the CCM user license is required, allowing the two layers to be reused by other eligible user groups.
