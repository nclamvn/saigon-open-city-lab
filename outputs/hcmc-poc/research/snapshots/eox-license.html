Direct Integration & Service Endpoints ​

Feel free to use the provided service endpoints (WMTS or WMS) directly in your application. Just make sure to follow the license conditions which are the attribution requirements for the corresponding year and non-commercial usage (2018 - 2025). The maximum request size is limited to 4096px, but you may stitch multiple requests to fit your needs.

For detailed technical integration guidelines and service capabilities, please refer to the Usage Section in our documentation.
